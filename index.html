GateKey Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · App: GateKey (com.pantrx.gatekey) · Published by Pantrx

GateKey lets you open the gates and doors on your Induct account from your phone. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and what stays on your phone.

The short version

  • GateKey has no servers of its own. Pantrx does not receive, store or sell your information.
  • The app talks only to Induct (which runs your building's gate system) and Induct's sign-in provider, Clerk.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in GateKey.

Information the app uses

  • Your Induct email and password. Entered when you sign in and sent directly to Induct's sign-in service (Clerk) over an encrypted connection. GateKey does not store your password. The sign-in service gives the app a session token, which is kept on your phone so you stay signed in.
  • Your precise location. Read when you open a gate and sent to Induct with that request, as the Induct app does; Induct may use it to check you are near the gate. While you have "Watch for arrival" running, GateKey reads your location to work out how far you are from the gate. That calculation happens on your phone, and those readings are not sent anywhere. Location is never collected in the background unless you started a watch, and a watch stops by itself after opening or alerting you, or after an hour unless you choose to keep it running.
  • Your gates. The names and identifiers of the gates and sites your Induct account can open, and the site's address location (used as the default arrival point), are fetched from Induct and stored on your phone.
  • Your settings. Your chosen gate, arrival distances and switches are stored on your phone.

Who receives information

  • Induct receives your sign-in session and your location when you open a gate, and provides your list of gates. Induct's own privacy policy applies to what it holds about you.
  • Clerk (Induct's sign-in provider) receives your email and password when you sign in, and may process technical details such as device and app version as part of signing in, under Induct's and Clerk's policies.
  • Google Play may collect crash and performance data under Google's policies if you have that enabled on your device.

Nobody else receives information from GateKey. Pantrx does not sell or share your information.

Keeping and deleting information

Everything GateKey stores lives on your phone. Signing out removes your gates, gate location and session from the app; uninstalling removes everything. Because Pantrx holds no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To access or delete information Induct holds about you, contact Induct.

Security

All network connections use HTTPS. Other apps on your phone cannot open your gate through GateKey without you confirming, unless you turn on "Let other apps open the gate without asking" in Settings.

Children

GateKey is intended for adults (18+) who hold an Induct account for their building.

Independence from Induct

GateKey is an independent app by Pantrx. It is not made, affiliated with or endorsed by Induct.
